# Code of conduct

Mina is maintained by a small group of people working on a narrow, security-sensitive problem.
We want participation to be straightforward and respectful for everyone.

## Expectations

- Be courteous and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep discussion on the technical and governance substance of the project.
- Respect the privacy of analysts and organisations who use or evaluate Mina. Do not post
  identifying details, logs or telemetry from real deployments in issues or pull requests.
- Follow the responsible-disclosure process in `SECURITY.md` for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information,
and sustained disruption of discussion are not acceptable in any project space.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at the address in the repository profile. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict or remove
participation for behaviour that violates this document.

## Scope

This document applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any communication made on behalf of the project.
